✨ Main Features

Real ping to the game server. AionPingMeter talks to the same servers your game character connects to, so the number you see reflects the actual trip your inputs take.

Clear lag diagnosis. When your ping spikes, the overlay helps you separate a local problem (computer under heavy load) from a network problem (Wi-Fi dropouts, crowded router) from a server problem (everyone on your world is affected).

🧭 How To Read The Numbers

You do not need a manual to understand the overlay, but a quick guide helps you react correctly when the numbers move.

  • Steady low numbers mean your connection to the server is healthy. Combat should feel responsive and skills should land when you press them.
  • Brief jumps that settle back down usually mean a small hiccup on the way to the server — a busy router, a congested Wi-Fi channel, or a background download on your network.
  • Numbers that rise and stay high suggest a persistent network problem between you and the game server. Try a wired connection or restart your router.
  • Numbers that climb while your computer is busy often point to your own machine struggling rather than your internet. Closing heavy background programs can help.
  • Numbers that jump for everyone at the same time usually mean the game server is under strain and no local fix will help.
